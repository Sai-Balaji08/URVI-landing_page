The Gap We See

The Problem

Millions of people care deeply about the planet — but caring alone isn't enough. The infrastructure to channel that care into consistent, verifiable action is missing.

💚

The Will Exists

People genuinely want to take environmental action — but don't know where or how to start.

🔍

No Discovery System

Local environmental events and drives are scattered, hard to find, and poorly promoted.

📊

Zero Tracking

After participating, there's no way to measure, record, or visualise the actual impact created.

🏅

No Recognition

Individual contributions go unnoticed — no scores, no validation, no sense of progress.

Our Answer

The Solution – URVI

URVI bridges the gap between environmental intent and measurable impact through a simple, rewarding loop.

🗺️

Discover & Participate

Find verified environmental events near you — tree plantations, river clean-ups, awareness drives — and join in one tap.

📸

Upload Proof

Submit photos or check-ins as evidence of your participation. Every contribution is verified and logged permanently.

📈

Track Your Impact

Watch your personal impact grow in real time — trees planted, water saved, air improved. Your effort, made visible.

Measure What Matters

The Impact System

Every action you take earns an impact score across three pillars of our planet's health.

🌳

Land

Green Score

Earned through tree planting, soil restoration, urban gardening, and land conservation activities.

💧

Water

Blue Score

Earned through river clean-ups, water conservation drives, wetland restoration, and coastal protection.

🌬️

Air

Air Score

Earned through reducing emissions, planting air-purifying flora, and participating in pollution awareness campaigns.
